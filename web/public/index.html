    How to play

    1. One player creates a room and sends the code or the link. The other joins. The host starts the duel.
    2. You stand at opposite ends of a boxcar roof, seven metres apart, while the train rolls through the desert.
    3. Each round: holster, then WAIT FOR IT… At a random moment: DRAW!
    4. The first to hit the other wins the round. The loser's hat flies off.
    5. Shoot before DRAW! and you lose the round.
    6. Duck or lean out of the way and their shot can miss. Six bullets a round.
    7. Everyone has three hats. Lose them all and you lose the duel. Then duel again, same room.

    Controls

    • Mouse: aim and click to shoot. Hold A / D to lean, S to duck.
    • Phone or tablet: tap your opponent to shoot. Hold the arrow buttons to lean and duck.
    • Webcam: point a finger gun at the screen and drop your thumb to fire. Lower your hand to holster. Space re-centres the aim.

    Draw times are taken on each player's own screen from the moment DRAW! appears there, so a slower connection does not decide who was faster.
