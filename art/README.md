# Game art

Everything here is generated by the scripts in `blender/ww/`. Original work, no third-party assets.

## Layout

- `blender/wildwest_enemies.blend` enemy roster, horses, all character actions
- `blender/wildwest_world.blend` diorama (both trains, track, terrain, town, cast) plus an asset library laid out at x > 400
- `blender/wildwest_chunks.blend` the 38 world chunks, a demo run and the guide's test run
- `blender/ww/` the generator
- `export/` FBX for Unreal, GLB for quick viewing. `SK_` = skeletal mesh with animations, `SM_` = static mesh
- `previews/` renders

## Enemies (`export/characters`, `export/horses`)

| Enemy | Use | Weapon |
|---|---|---|
| Bandit | riders, boarders | revolver |
| Gunslinger | elite | two long revolvers |
| Rifleman | window and roof shooter | lever rifle |
| Deputy | crooked lawman reskin | revolver |
| Dynamiter | thrower | dynamite bundle |
| Heavy | gatling crew, 1.1x scale | shotgun |
| Boss | showdown, 1.06x scale | long revolver, poncho |

About 3.6k to 4.5k triangles each. Horses 2.3k, four coats.

All cowboys share one skeleton (UE mannequin style names). `SK_Bandit.fbx`, `SK_Heavy.fbx` and `SK_Boss.fbx` carry the full animation set. The other four FBX files are mesh only to keep the repo small: import them onto the Bandit skeleton. Every `.glb` has all animations.

Animations, 30 fps, in place:

- Movement: `idle walk run climb hit hit_aim death_back death_forward`
- Revolver: `aim_start aim shoot shoot_burst reload walk_shoot hip_aim hip_fire fan_fire kneel_aim kneel_shoot quickdraw showdown_idle`
- Two revolvers: `dual_aim dual_shoot`
- Cover: `cover_idle cover_popup cover_shoot cover_hide`
- Long guns: `rifle_idle rifle_aim_start rifle_aim rifle_shoot shotgun_shoot rifle_hit`
- Mounted: `ride_idle ride_gallop ride_aim_left ride_aim_right ride_shoot_left ride_shoot_right ride_death`
- Other: `throw gatling_fire`
- Horse: `horse_idle horse_walk horse_gallop horse_rear horse_death`

Notes:

- `aim_start` is 21 frames (0.7 s), matching the telegraph in PLAN.md.
- Bones `muzzle_r` / `muzzle_l` sit at the barrel tip. Spawn muzzle flashes and tracers there.
- Weapons are skinned to `weapon_r` / `weapon_l`, hats to `hat`. Swap in the `SM_` props on those bones if you want hats to fly off.
- Rider: attach the rider's root to the horse bone `saddle` with Z offset -90 cm (times the character scale). `ride_gallop` and `horse_gallop` are both 14 frames.
- Gatling crew: put `SK_Gatling` 97 cm in front of the Heavy, play `gatling_fire` with `gatling_spin`.

## Train (`export/train`)

Locomotive (4-4-0), tender, passenger car, boxcar, three flatcars (crates, gatling sandbag nest, logs), caboose. Two liveries: player (green) and `_Bandit` (red).

- Forward is -Y in Blender. Origin is on the rail top at the middle of each vehicle. Rail top is 0.53 m above ground.
- Coupler reach from origin, front/back, in metres: locomotive 5.4/5.1, tender 3.7, passenger 7.9, boxcar and flatcars 6.0, caboose 5.4.
- Every vehicle has `<name>_roll`: 24 frames = one wheel turn. Locomotive drivers, side rods, main rods, crossheads and bell all move. Set play rate from speed.
- Boxcars have `<name>_doors_open` (bones `door_l`, `door_r`) for bandits to pop out.
- Passenger car windows are real openings for window shooters. Roof walkways are about 3.9 m above the rail top.

## Track and terrain (`export/track`, `export/terrain`, `export/scenery`)

- `Track_Straight_20m` tiles end to end along +Y. Also a trestle piece, buffer stop, and `TelegraphSpan_30m` (pole plus wires to the next pole, instance every 30 m).
- `TerrainTile_A/B/C`: 60 m long, 260 m wide, origin at the start of the tile with x = 0 on the main line. All three share the same edge profile, so any order loops without gaps. Flat corridor for the main line and a second line at x = +7 m (the bandit train).
- Scatter: saguaros, barrel cactus, prickly pear, sagebrush, dead trees, rocks, tumbleweed, skull, fence. Landmarks: three mesas, two buttes, a rock arch.
- Procedural: `scenery.track_straight(mb, length)`, `scenery.terrain_tile(mb, seed)`, `scenery.mesa(mb, seed, width, depth, height)` give new variants from a seed.

## World chunks (`export/chunks`, `blender/wildwest_chunks.blend`)

Built to the Blender Chunk Design Guide: +X forward, origin at the start of the chunk on the main track, 50 m = 5000 uu long, track at Y = 0, rail top 0.53 m. Blender is right-handed, so the driver's left is +Y in Blender; Unreal's FBX import flips Y and you get the guide's +Y = right.

38 chunks, 3k to 8k polygons each. Each FBX holds four meshes (`_Ground`, `_Track`, `_SceneryNear`, `_SceneryMid`) plus sockets: `Connector_Start`, `Connector_End`, `Encounter_Left_01/02`, `Encounter_Right_01/02`, `Encounter_Ahead_01`, and `EnemyTrain_01` on side-track chunks. Empties are exported as `SOCKET_*`, which Unreal turns into static mesh sockets when they sit under a mesh (import with Combine Meshes on to get one mesh with all sockets). No trains, enemies or gameplay are baked in.

**Joints.** Every chunk end is one of four joint types with a fixed cross-section. B may follow A when `B.start_joint == A.end_joint`:

| Joint | Meaning | Chunks |
|---|---|---|
| `O` | open desert, single line | Flat, Rocky, Canyon, Cactus, Boneyard, Telegraph_Fence, Mesa, CurveL/R, SBendL/R, Gulch, all Landmarks: **any order** |
| `C` | inside a deep canyon | CanyonDeep_Entry (O to C), CanyonDeep_Mid / CurveL / CurveR (C to C), CanyonDeep_Exit (C to O) |
| `T` | inside a tunnel | Tunnel_Entry, Tunnel_Mid / CurveL, Tunnel_Exit |
| `OD` | enemy side track alongside at 7 m left | SideTrack_Start, SideTrack_Mid / CurveL / CurveR, SideTrack_End |

The build measures the ground height at every legal pairing: worst gap is 0.0 m for all four joints. Rails, ballast and telegraph wires line up too (poles sit at 12.5 m and 37.5 m, never on a joint).

**Curves.** Curves turn 10 degrees per chunk, S-bends swing 6 degrees out and back. Curvature eases to zero at both ends, so position, heading and curvature all match at a joint. Chain several for a long bend. To place chunk B, put it at A's `Connector_End` world transform (position and yaw). With the train fixed at the origin, move and rotate the whole chunk chain by the inverse of the track pose at the train's current distance; `centreline_every_5m` in `chunks.json` gives that pose.

**`chunks.json`** lists, per chunk: joints, `end_connector`, centreline samples, marker positions, `rigs` to spawn (water tower, station bell, windmill, with the action to play), `events` (`duck` with distance along the chunk, `dark` for tunnels, `trestle`, `narrow`, `side_track`), and a `weight` for random selection (landmarks are rare). `guide_test_order` is the Flat, Canyon, Rocky, Flat, Rocky, Canyon test from the guide; `demo_order` is a 1.7 km run using every chunk type. Both are laid out in the blend file.

Riding lanes 10 to 18 m either side of the track are kept clear in all `O` chunks. Deep canyon, tunnel, gulch and arch chunks have no side encounters (their markers are left out).

Rebuild headless:

```bash
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup --python art/blender/build_chunks.py
```

New variants: add a line to `RECIPES` in `blender/ww/chunks.py` (joints, shape, ground options, dressing recipe).

## Town, set pieces, props (`export/buildings`, `export/setpieces`, `export/props`)

- Buildings face -Y, origin at the front wall: Station (platform edge at y = -7.5), GeneralStore, Sheriff, Bank, Hotel, Shack, Church, Barn, Outhouse.
- Rigged: `WaterTower` (`WaterTower_spout_swing` is the duck obstacle, track 5.5 m from the tower on its -X side), `Windmill_spin`, `StationBell_ring` (shoot to depart), `Saloon_doors_swing`.
- Duck obstacles: `LowGantry`, `TunnelPortal`, `TunnelSegment_20m`. Clearance is 5.9 m above ground.
- Tutorial: `BottleCrate`, `Bottle_Green`, `Bottle_Brown`, `TinCan`, `StartSign`.
- Clutter: crates, barrel, bench, lamp post, lantern, hitching post, trough, hay bale, sack, wanted board, signpost, wagon.

## Player gun and FX (`export/fx`)

- `SK_PlayerRevolver`: first-person arm and revolver. Bones `gun`, `cylinder`, `hammer`, `trigger`, `muzzle`. Actions `fp_idle fp_fire fp_dry_fire fp_reload fp_draw fp_holster fp_spin`.
- `Tracer_Player`, `Tracer_Enemy` (fat and slow, for the dodgeable shots), three muzzle flashes, bullet, casing, slug (these three are 10x life size), dust / smoke / steam puffs for mesh particles, glass shard, wood splinter.
- Flash, tracer and lit-window materials are emissive.

## Unreal import

- Characters and trains face -Y in Blender. Rotate the mesh -90 yaw in the Blueprint if needed.
- Materials are flat colors, one per palette entry, named `WW_<color>`. No textures.

## Regenerate

In Blender's Python console:

```python
import sys; sys.path.insert(0, "/path/to/repo/art/blender")
import ww.build_all as b; b.build(); b.export_all("/path/to/repo/art/export")      # enemies
import ww.build_world as w; w.build(); w.export_all("/path/to/repo/art/export")    # world
```

Edit `variants.py` to reskin enemies, `train.LIVERY` for train colors, `core.PAL` for the palette.
